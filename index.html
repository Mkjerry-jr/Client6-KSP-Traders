Our Story

A family coconut business that never stopped visiting the farm

KSP Traders has been sourcing and supplying coconuts to Chennai since 1998. We work directly with growers we know by name, husking and grading every coconut by hand before it leaves our yard, and pressing our own coconut oil cold from copra we dry ourselves. What began as a single delivery route has grown into a steady wholesale and retail supply for shops, hotels, and home kitchens across the city.
